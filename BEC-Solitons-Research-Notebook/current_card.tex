\section{Current card 0.1: What is the 1D condensate field?}

\noindent\textbf{One question:} in the equation below, what does $|\psi(x,t)|^2$ count?
\[
i\hbar\partial_t\psi=-\frac{\hbar^2}{2m}\partial_x^2\psi
+g_{1D}|\psi|^2\psi.
\]
For now this is the model to read, not an equation we are deriving from atoms. The first check is the normalization $\int dx\,|\psi(x,t)|^2=N$: $|\psi|^2$ is number per unit length and $\psi$ has units $\mathrm{length}^{-1/2}$. We will verify these units and interpret the terms together before proceeding.

\noindent\textbf{Checkpoint:} if $|\psi|^2$ is line density, what units must $g_{1D}$ have so that $g_{1D}|\psi|^2$ is an energy? We stop here until this is comfortable.
