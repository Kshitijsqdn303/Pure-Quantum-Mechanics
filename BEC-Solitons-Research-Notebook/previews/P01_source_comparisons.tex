\paragraph{S\"ohn: exact cluster versus leading GPE.}
For an attractive 1D delta gas with $N$ bosons and total momentum $P$, the Bethe cluster energy is
\[
E_{\rm Bethe}(N,P)=\frac{P^2}{2Nm}
-\frac{m g_{1D}^2}{24\hbar^2}N(N^2-1).
\]
The leading stationary GPE calculation at the same coupling and $P=0$ gives
\[
E_{\rm GP}=-\frac{mg_{1D}^2N^3}{24\hbar^2},\qquad
\frac{E_{\rm Bethe}(N,0)}{E_{\rm GP}}=1-\frac1{N^2}.
\]
This comparison uses the leading GPE functional with $N$ in the cubic term; a finite-$N$ Hartree calculation instead retains $N-1$ and will be treated separately. The exact state has a delocalized centre of mass when $P$ is sharp. Source: S\"ohn Chapter 4, especially Equation (4.20).

\paragraph{Balakrishnan--Satija: hard-core order-parameter density.}
In the paper's spin-coherent approximation let $b_j=S_j^+$ and $n_j=\frac12-S_j^z$. For a spin angle $\theta$ and phase $\phi$, $\langle S^+\rangle=\frac12\sin\theta\,e^{i\phi}$ and the site filling is $\rho=(1-\cos\theta)/2$. Hence
\[
\rho_s=|\langle b\rangle|^2=\frac14\sin^2\theta=\rho(1-\rho).
\]
The curve vanishes at $\rho=0$ and $1$, peaks at $\rho=1/2$, and is invariant under $\rho\mapsto1-\rho$. It is an order-parameter density in the spin-coherent approximation, not a claim of exact off-diagonal long-range order for a thermodynamic 1D gas. Source: Balakrishnan--Satija Section 5.2, Equation (19).
