\section{A3: Creation and annihilation operators}

For each orbital $j$, define operators that change its occupation:
\[
\hat a_j|\ldots,n_j,\ldots\rangle
=\sqrt{n_j}|\ldots,n_j-1,\ldots\rangle,
\]
\[
\hat a_j^\dagger|\ldots,n_j,\ldots\rangle
=\sqrt{n_j+1}|\ldots,n_j+1,\ldots\rangle.
\]
They satisfy the bosonic algebra
\[
[\hat a_i,\hat a_j^\dagger]=\delta_{ij},
\qquad
[\hat a_i,\hat a_j]=
[\hat a_i^\dagger,\hat a_j^\dagger]=0.
\]

Starting from $\hat a_j|0\rangle=0$, normalized number states are
\[
\boxed{
|n_0,n_1,\ldots\rangle
=\prod_j\frac{(\hat a_j^\dagger)^{n_j}}{\sqrt{n_j!}}|0\rangle.
}
\]
The square roots follow from
\[
[\hat a,(\hat a^\dagger)^n]=n(\hat a^\dagger)^{n-1}.
\]
Indeed,
\[
\hat a|n\rangle
=\frac{n}{\sqrt{n!}}(\hat a^\dagger)^{n-1}|0\rangle
=\sqrt n|n-1\rangle,
\]
and similarly
\[
\hat a^\dagger|n\rangle=\sqrt{n+1}|n+1\rangle.
\]

The mode-number operator is
\[
\hat n_j=\hat a_j^\dagger\hat a_j,
\qquad
\hat n_j|\ldots,n_j,\ldots\rangle
=n_j|\ldots,n_j,\ldots\rangle.
\]
Therefore the total number operator is
\[
\boxed{\hat N=\sum_j\hat a_j^\dagger\hat a_j.}
\]

The combination $\hat a_i^\dagger\hat a_j$ transfers a boson from orbital
$j$ to orbital $i$:
\[
\hat a_i^\dagger\hat a_j
|\ldots,n_i,\ldots,n_j,\ldots\rangle
=\sqrt{n_j}\sqrt{n_i+1}
|\ldots,n_i+1,\ldots,n_j-1,\ldots\rangle.
\]
It conserves the total particle number. Thus creation and annihilation
operators need not describe literal production or destruction of atoms; they
are operators for changing mode occupations.

For noninteracting bosons,
\[
\hat H_0=\sum_j\epsilon_j\hat a_j^\dagger\hat a_j,
\]
so
\[
\hat H_0|n_0,n_1,\ldots\rangle
=\left(\sum_j\epsilon_jn_j\right)|n_0,n_1,\ldots\rangle.
\]
The factor $\sqrt{n_j+1}$ in the creation amplitude is the algebraic origin of
bosonic enhancement.
